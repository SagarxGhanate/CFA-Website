Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you train?

We offer classes for three age groups: Toddlers (2–5 years), Kids (6–14 years), and Adults (15+). Each program is tailored to the developmental stage of the age group.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Yes! Contact us to book a trial session and experience CFA training firsthand. We want you to feel comfortable before committing.

How long is each class?

Each class is approximately 55–60 minutes, following our structured format: 10 min warmup → Skill 1 → Skill 2 → Workouts → Cooldown & stretching.

Is it safe for young children?

Absolutely. Safety is our #1 priority. All exercises for toddlers and kids are age-appropriate, supervised, and performed in padded areas. Our coaches are trained specifically for youth fitness.

What should I bring?

Comfortable athletic wear, a water bottle, and a towel. All equipment is provided at our studio. Chalk is available for adult classes.

How do I get a price quote?

Pricing is customized based on age group, class type (group/personal), and session frequency. DM us on Instagram or fill out the membership form for a personalized quote.
