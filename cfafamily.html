Founder

Zaid Palla

Founder & Head Coach

Zaid founded CFA in 2023 with a vision to bring calisthenics training to everyone — from toddlers discovering movement for the first time to adults mastering advanced skills. His passion for bodyweight training and commitment to safe, structured coaching has built CFA into Mumbai's trusted calisthenics studio.

Based in Byculla, Mumbai, Zaid leads every training program with a safety-first approach, ensuring proper progressions and age-appropriate techniques for all students.
